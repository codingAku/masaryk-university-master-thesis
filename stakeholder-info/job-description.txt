# Job Roles Description

## Assembler
* Build instrument
* Does not do any analytical measurement
* Toucan roles: Operator

## Profiler
* Specific job role for OES instrument
* Profiler builds internal optics of iSpark
* Toucan roles: Operator, Profiler

## Manufacturing Technician
* Is responsible for QC/QA test during manufacturing
* Operates with Toucan manufacturing environment and OXSAS on daily basis
* Does not require access to MK
* Toucan roles: Operator, Manufacturing engineer, Analytical expert
* Note: Toucan roles must be updated

## Analytical Expert
* Is responsible for managing manufacturing qualities and samples
* Troubleshoot and accepts NOK and OK with Warnings curves in QA and Calibrations
* Manage statistics and update limits in MK
* Uses elevated screens in manufacturing (Full MVR)
* Responsible for derogations of analytical results based on customer use cases
* On XR also responsible for creation of Monochromator list for 9900
* Toucan roles: Operator, Manufacturing engineer, Analytical expert

## Calibrator
* Responsible for instrument calibration - MVR, UniQuant, QuantAs
* Toucan roles: Operator, Manufacturing engineer, Analytical expert
* Note: Toucan roles must be updated

## Operation Engineer
* Support for manufacturing team
* Troubleshoot HW, analytical and software issues
* Uses Manufacturing, Statistics and MK parts of Toucan
* Is responsible for managing HW parts and QC stuff in MK
* Toucan roles: Operator, Manufacturing engineer, Analytical expert
* Note: Toucan roles must be updated

## Sample Manager
* Responsible for replacing of obsolete samples
* Also create samples for XR manufacturing (mixing them in laboratory)
* Toucan roles: Operator, Manufacturing engineer, Analytical expert

## Application Specialist
* Create new analytical applications for our instruments
* Uses elevated screens in manufacturing (Full MVR) to create new qualities and MK
* Toucan roles: Operator, Analytical expert, Analytical specialist

## Sales Representative
* Manages discussion with our customers about their use case and proposed configuration
* Works in Define instrument (internal) or external LCS (contractors)
* Toucan roles: Sales engineer

## Marketing Specialist
* Responsible for check of instrument defined by sales
* Responsible for NSR management - both analytical and HW
* Toucan roles: Sales admin, Marketing analytical specialist
